# Daedalon model work

For every new OBJ model, follow `docs/ADDING_OBJ_MODELS.md`. Reuse the shared
mesh, statue-profile, material-variant, generator, and test paths; do not create
per-material geometry or hand-copy generated assets. Add and validate one new
model as a pilot before activating the next batch.

## Model ownership

Oliver confirmed that the models he supplies for Daedalon, including future
models, are his own Meshy creations and may be included in the public Daedalon
repository. Record that provenance without asking for the same confirmation
again, unless he identifies a different source or conflicting evidence appears.
Preserve existing third-party licences and attribution notices; this confirmation
does not reclassify separately credited third-party assets.
